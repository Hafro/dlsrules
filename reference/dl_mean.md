# Mean length or age in the catch above length or age of first capture

Mean length or age in the catch above length or age of first capture

## Usage

``` r
dl_mean(x, y, first)
```

## Arguments

- x:

  length or age

- y:

  bin count of length or age, normally observed catches

- first:

  length or age of first capture

## Value

A vector of the mean length or age of the catch
